# methods

Six samples (ROBO1 KO n=3, AAVS1 control n=3) were sequenced on a NextSeq 2000 (51 bp paired-end, 15.3-18.9 M read pairs per sample). Adapters, poly-G tails and low quality bases were trimmed with fastp v1.3.7 (--detect_adapter_for_pe --trim_poly_g --length_required 25) and reports were combined with MultiQC v1.35.

Reads were quantified with Salmon v2.8.0 against GENCODE v50 transcripts (Ensembl 116). The v50 transcript fasta also includes ~26,000 transcripts from alternate haplotypes, so it was filtered to the 644,292 transcripts in the primary annotation GTF before building the index. The index was built with k = 25 since reads are 51 bp. No genome decoys were used because of memory limits (24 GB RAM). Quantification was run with -l A --validateMappings --gcBias (--validateMappings has no effect in Salmon 2.x because selective alignment is the default). All samples were detected as ISR (dUTP stranded).

Transcript counts were summarized to gene level with tximport v1.40.0, and gene symbols were taken from the GENCODE v50 GTF. Genes with at least 10 reads in at least 3 samples were kept (16,912 genes). Differential expression was done with DESeq2 v1.52.0 in R 4.6.0 (Bioconductor 3.23) using design ~ condition with AAVS1 as the reference. P values were adjusted with Benjamini-Hochberg and fold changes were shrunk with apeglm v1.34.0. Genes with padj < 0.05 and |log2FC| > 1 (shrunk) were called significant, and genes with padj < 0.05 regardless of fold change are reported separately. PCA (top 500 genes) and sample distances were computed on vst transformed counts (blind = TRUE).

Gene set enrichment was done with fgsea v1.38.0 (eps = 0, gene set size 15-500) using MSigDB 2026.1.Hs gene sets from msigdbr v26.1.1 (Hallmark, GO BP, Reactome, KEGG legacy). We also tested a set of GBM signatures (min size 10): Neftel et al. 2019 meta-modules (Table S2, old gene symbols updated with org.Hs.eg.db), Verhaak 2010 subtypes, 3CA glioma metaprograms and glioma/embryonic stem cell sets (Beier, Ben-Porath, Wong, Malta, Ramalho, Zheng). Genes were first ranked by apeglm shrunk log2FC. Since apeglm shrinks about 73% of genes to |log2FC| < 0.05, a lot of the ranked list is tied, so we also ranked by the DESeq2 Wald statistic and report both. Redundant GO BP and Reactome terms were collapsed with collapsePathways.

For co-targets we took genes up in KO (padj < 0.05) with at least one cell surface annotation. Each gene got a surface score of 0-3, one point each for plasma membrane location in HPA v25.1, surface label in SURFY and high confidence (category 1) in CSPA. Tier 1 genes have log2FC > 1 and a score of 2 or more, tier 2 genes have a score of 2 or more with any fold change, and tier 3 genes have a score of 1. Normal tissue expression was taken from GTEx v10 median TPM (max over brain regions, and max over all other tissues excluding cell lines) and from HPA normal tissue IHC (highest level in brain regions, excluding uncertain reliability). Within each tier genes were ranked by the average percentile of log2FC, -log10 padj, TPM in KO, surface score, and low brain and other tissue expression. Existing drugs and antibodies were pulled from DGIdb (only interactions with a listed interaction type) and from our own list of known CAR-T/ADC targets. Secreted genes were DE genes (padj < 0.05, |log2FC| > 1) with an HPA secretome location annotated as secreted.

Code is in 01_qc.sh, 02_quant.sh, 03_de.R, 04_pathways.R and 05_targets.R. R package versions are in results/sessionInfo_*.txt.
